New to AI? The whole skill in 2 minutes.

An AI's answer is only as good as your prompt. Here is the recipe every PromptRunic prompt is already built on — you just fill in the blanks.
1 · Role

Tell the AI who to be — "Act as an experienced career coach…"

2 · Task

Say exactly what you want done.

3 · Context

Give real details: names, numbers, your situation.

4 · Format

How you want the answer — a table, 5 bullets, an email.

5 · Constraints

Limits and tone — "Under 150 words, friendly, no jargon."

Weak prompt"Write a resume."
Strong prompt"Act as a professional resume writer. Rewrite my experience as a marketing manager into 5 achievement bullets with measurable results. One page, confident but not boastful."
